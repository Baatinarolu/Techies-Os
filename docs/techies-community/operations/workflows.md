# TECHIES COMMUNITY — Operating Workflows

## New member

1. Member lands in Announcements.
2. Welcome message links to rules, directory, and access request.
3. Member confirms rules and chooses interests/timezone.
4. Admin approves Tier 2 and sends Lounge invite.
5. Room owners review Tier 3 requests against role/project and review date.
6. Leadership manually invites Tier 4 members.
7. Admin checks stale requests after 48 hours and reviews dynamic access monthly.

## Task created

1. Author posts `TASK-[number]`, outcome, owner, contributors, due date, priority, and definition of done.
2. Owner replies `ACCEPTED`, `CLARIFY`, or `DECLINE — reason`.
3. Create/link the canonical task record.
4. Notify only the owner and contributors; link to the project topic if relevant.
5. At due date, request `DONE`, `BLOCKED`, or revised date.
6. Copy blockers to Project Follow-Up and summarize resolved work weekly.

## Project milestone

1. Lead posts `MILESTONE` with project ID, evidence, status, next milestone, and owner.
2. Record the decision/change in Project Command.
3. Post a compact status card in Follow-Up.
4. For AMBER/RED, capture blocker, impact, and recovery plan.
5. For COMPLETE, draft a Showcase post; admin checks consent and confidentiality.
6. Publish only after approval; include the milestone in the weekly digest.

## Feedback submitted

1. Member uses the group or private form and selects suggestion, complaint, safety, or access.
2. Assign `FB-[number]` and acknowledge receipt.
3. Triage urgency, confidentiality, and anonymity preference.
4. Route to room owner or leadership.
5. Track `Received → Reviewing → Planned/Declined/Resolved`.
6. Update the reporter privately and publish only redacted aggregate summaries.

## Event posted

1. Post title, purpose, host, UTC and local time, link, RSVP method, and accessibility notes.
2. Publish in Events & Calendar; mirror only major events to Announcements.
3. Remind at 7 days, 24 hours, and 1 hour.
4. Keep the RSVP source of truth in the calendar/form.
5. Post resources and a feedback prompt after the event.
