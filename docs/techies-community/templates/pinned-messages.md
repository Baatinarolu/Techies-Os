# Copy-ready pinned messages

## Global rules — pin in `☕ Lounge`

> **WELCOME TO TECHIES COMMUNITY**
>
> This is a professional but human space for learning, building, sharing, and collaborating remotely.
>
> 1. Be useful and respectful. Challenge ideas, not people.
> 2. Use the right room. Check the directory before posting.
> 3. Never share passwords, API keys, private client data, HR details, financial records, or sensitive security information here.
> 4. No harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, spam, impersonation, or unsolicited mass promotion.
> 5. Credit collaborators and sources. Label AI-generated work where relevant.
> 6. Include timezone information for events and clear context for links/files.
> 7. Report safety, harassment, or confidential concerns privately to an admin or through Community Feedback.
> 8. Moderation may escalate from reminder → warning → mute → removal → ban. Serious issues may skip steps.
>
> By participating, you agree to these rules. Material updates will be announced in Announcements.

## Founder's Office — pin in `🏛 Founder's Office`

> **CONFIDENTIALITY AND ESCALATION**
>
> Treat messages, files, names, financial information, people matters, and decisions here as confidential. Do not forward, screenshot, export, or quote content outside this room without permission.
>
> Invite only the people needed for each agenda/case. Prefix decisions with `DECISION`, include the owner and effective date, and copy safe operational decisions to the correct team room.
>
> Escalate immediately to the designated founder/admin: threats, safeguarding concerns, fraud, credential exposure, legal risk, serious security incidents, or material financial risk.
>
> Keep HR cases in the restricted HR process. State concerns with evidence and a proposed alternative; once decided, record the owner and review date.
